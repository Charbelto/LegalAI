Harmonised standards are technical specifications developed by European Standardisation
Organisations (CEN-CENELEC in this case) that provide a presumption of conformity with EU
legislation. When a standard is cited in the Official Journal of the EU (OJEU), following it
creates a rebuttable presumption that you comply with the corresponding legal requirements. For
the AI Act, this means demonstrating compliance becomes significantly more straightforward.
