Quick Answer:
The EU AI Act’s enforcement timeline was substantially rewritten on May 7, 2026, when the EU institutions reached a provisional agreement on the Digital Omnibus on AI. High-risk AI system obligations (Annex III) are now deferred to December 2, 2027 — 16 months later than originally scheduled.
August 2, 2026 — today — is the date Article 50 transparency obligations, GPAI penalty powers, and full national market surveillance authority activate.
Three new compliance deadlines also emerged from the deal.
